Problem trigger: Tommy is a college student who often stays up until 2 AM finishing assignments. His irregular schedule leaves him exhausted in morning classes and irritable with his roommates. After oversleeping and missing another lecture, Tommy realizes he needs help establishing a healthier routine. He realizes that he also finds it easier to stick to goals when held accountable by others so he wants others to assist him on his journey to improving sleep habits.

1. Setting a routine and accountability partners (UI: Bedtime/Wake setup page) Tommy opens ShutEye Showdown and navigates to the "Sleep Schedule" page He enters his goals: bedtime at 11:00 PM and wake-up at 7:00 AM for weekdays, and 11:00 PM to 10:00 AM on weekends. Also, for all the times he set he selected the "strict" 5 minute tolerance because he wants to rigoursly commit to his sleep schedule. He taps Submit, and the app confirms that his new routine is saved. This gives Tommy a clear, structured starting point for improving his sleep hygiene. His roommate, Timothy does the Timothy, with the Timothy bedtimes and wake up times.

2. Tommy sets up an accountability partner Timothy who also uses the ShutEyeShowdown app and he allows Timothy to get a report for Bedtime fails, and sets the frequency to Immediate. So every bed time failure will show up as a report on the "Reports Inbox" page for Timothy.

3. Tommy also sets up a competition with Timothy for 11/3-11/6.

4. Tommy than reports that on 10/27 he slept at 11:19 PM which is over five minutes past his bed time, which is a fail. He can see this fail in the analytics page which shows the progress for the week. But, his roommate forgets to report any sleep events for the competition.

5. Tommy checks the leaderboard and it currently shows him tied but that is because users get 1 point for successfull sleep events, 0 for unsuccessful, and at the end of the competition scores will be decremented by 1 for each unreported sleep event. Also both users can see how much sleep events they have reported, and each unreported sleep event will result in their scores decrementing in the end. When the competition has ended Tommy is the winner since Timothy did not report any sleep events.

Outcome: Tommy felt pushed to follow his bed time and wake up time because he had motivation through a competition against his roommate, and he also wanted to send his failures to his roomate so that he gets a log of his bedtime failures weekly via email. Additionally, he didn't feel the temptation to not report his failures since failures do not decrement ones score while not reporting does.

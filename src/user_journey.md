Problem trigger: Alex is a college student who often stays up until 2 AM finishing assignments. His irregular schedule leaves him exhausted in morning classes and irritable with his roommates. After oversleeping and missing another lecture, Alex realizes he needs help establishing a healthier routine. He realizes that he also finds it easier to stick to goals when held accountable by others so he wants others to assist him on his journey to improving sleep habits.

1. Setting a routine and accountability partners (UI: Bedtime/Wake setup page) Alex opens ShutEye Showdown and navigates to the “Setting Up Bedtimes and Wake-up Times” page He enters his goals: bedtime at 11:00 PM and wake-up at 7:00 AM for weekdays, and 11:00 PM to 10:00 AM on weekends. He taps Submit, and the app confirms that his new routine is saved. This gives Alex a clear, structured starting point for improving his sleep hygiene. His roommate, Sam does the same, with the same bedtimes and wake up times.

2. Alex sets up an accountability partner Sam who also uses the ShutEyeShowdown app and he allows Sam to get an email notification for Bedtime fails, and sets the frequency to Weekly. So every week Sam will get an email that shows all of the times Alex failed to sleep on time for the past week.

3. Alex also sets up a competition with Sam for 10/20-10/21.

4. Alex than reports that on 10/20 he slept before his bed time but his roommate forgets to report both his wake up time and sleep time.

5. Alex checks the leaderboard to see that he is leading and he ends the competition and he was the winner.

Outcome: Alex felt pushed to follow his bed time and wake up time because he had motivation through a competition against his roommate, and he also wanted to send his failures to his roomate so that he gets a log of his bedtime failures weekly via email.
